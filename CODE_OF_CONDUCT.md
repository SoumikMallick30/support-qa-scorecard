# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and project outcomes.
- Welcome different support and QA practices.
- Protect customer privacy and use anonymized examples.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical.
