## Critical Errors

A critical error may override the numerical QA score depending on the organization's policy.

Examples include:

- Sharing sensitive customer information incorrectly
- Skipping mandatory identity or account verification
- Providing materially incorrect information that could harm the customer
- Unauthorized account changes or actions
- Serious compliance or security violations
- Intentional call/chat avoidance or manipulation
